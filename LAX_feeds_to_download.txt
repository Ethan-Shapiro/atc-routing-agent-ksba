1. Ground Operations & Routing
This data trains your agent on taxiway routing, hold-short instructions, runway crossings, and pushback clearances.

KLAX Clearance Delivery (120.350): Essential for learning how initial IFR routes (SIDs) and squawk codes are issued before a plane even moves.

KLAX Ground (North) (121.650): Taxi routing for the northern runways and terminals 1-3, and the northern side of Tom Bradley International Terminal (TBIT).

KLAX Ground (South) (121.750): Taxi routing for the southern runways and terminals 4-8, plus the south side of TBIT.

KLAX Ground (Metering) (121.400): Crucial for learning how LAX meters aircraft out of the alleyways to prevent taxiway gridlock during heavy pushes.

2. Local Control (Tower)
This data trains your agent on runway management, takeoff/landing clearances, line-up-and-wait timing, and wake turbulence spacing.

KLAX Tower (North) #1 (133.900): Handles all takeoff and landing clearances for 24L/24R and 6L/6R.

KLAX Tower (South) #1 (119.800 / 120.950): Handles all takeoff and landing clearances for 25L/25R and 7L/7R.

3. TRACON (Arrivals & Departures)
This data trains your agent on airborne vectoring, speed assignments, altitude steps, and sequencing aircraft into the final approach fix.

KLAX Final App (North) (128.500): The Stadium sector. Vectors aircraft onto the final approach course for the North complex.

KLAX Final App (South) #1 (124.900): The Downey sector. Vectors aircraft onto the final approach course for the South complex.

SOCAL Dep (Manhattan Sector) (124.300): Captures the initial climb-out and handoffs for departures heading Southwest through Northeast.

SOCAL Dep (Malibu Sector W/NW Dep) #1 (125.200): Captures the initial climb-out and handoffs for departures heading West and Northwest.
